1. Migration & Import Apps
These apps help businesses move from platforms like WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, Etsy, eBay, Amazon, etc., to Shopify.
- Product & Inventory Migration – Import products, descriptions, images, and stock levels.
- Order & Customer Data Migration – Transfer order history, customer info, and reviews.
- SEO Migration – Automatically redirect old URLs and keep SEO rankings.
- Theme & Design Import – Convert layouts from other platforms into Shopify-compatible themes.

2. Multi-Channel Sync Apps
These apps allow merchants to sell on multiple platforms while using Shopify as the main hub.
- Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Walmart Sync – Manage products and orders across multiple marketplaces.
- Social Media Store Integration – Sell on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp.
- Real-Time Inventory Sync – Keep stock levels updated across different sales channels.
- Centralized Order Management – Process all orders in Shopify, even from external platforms.

3. Payment Gateway & Currency Apps
If a business moves from another platform, they might need new payment solutions.
- Multi-Currency Payments – Auto-detect and convert prices based on customer location.
- Third-Party Payment Gateway Integration – Connect Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay, etc.
- Subscription & Recurring Payments – For businesses that sell memberships or services.
- Buy Now, Pay Later Integration – Allow payments in installments (Afterpay, Klarna, etc.).

4. Marketing & SEO Apps for New Shopify Merchants
These apps help new merchants boost traffic and sales quickly after migration.
- SEO Optimization & Redirects – Automatically redirect old platform URLs to Shopify.
- Email & SMS Marketing Setup – Pre-built email templates for customer engagement.
- Google Shopping & Facebook Ads Integration – One-click setup for advertising.
- Abandoned Cart Recovery – Help new stores recover lost sales.

5. Store Customization & Builder Apps
Migrating users may need help setting up their Shopify store quickly.
- Page & Theme Builder – Drag-and-drop design tools for Shopify pages.
- Custom Product Pages – Allow merchants to design product pages easily.
- AI-Powered Store Setup – Auto-generate pages, images, and product descriptions.
- Personalization & Localization – Translate store content and customize layouts.

6. Dropshipping & Print-on-Demand Apps
Some merchants may move to Shopify to start dropshipping or print-on-demand businesses.
- AliExpress, Spocket, CJ Dropshipping Integrations – Easily import products.
- Print-on-Demand (POD) Setup – Connect Shopify with POD suppliers (Printify, Printful, etc.).
- Automated Order Fulfillment – Handle product sourcing, fulfillment, and shipping.

7. Wholesale & B2B Migration Apps
For businesses moving to Shopify to sell wholesale products or manage B2B customers.
- Bulk Pricing & Discounts – Set tiered pricing for different customer groups.
- Customer-Specific Catalogs – Show different products based on user login.
- Quote Request & Custom Orders – Allow B2B buyers to request bulk order quotes.

8. AI & Automation Apps for New Shopify Stores
These apps use AI to help new merchants automate tasks and improve store setup.
- AI-Powered Product Descriptions – Generate optimized product content.
- AI Chatbots & Customer Support – Automate customer interactions.
- AI Inventory Forecasting – Predict best-selling products and restock levels.
- Automated Social Media Posting – Schedule and post content automatically.
